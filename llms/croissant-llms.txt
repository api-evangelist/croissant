# Croissant

> Croissant is the workspace infrastructure layer for distributed teams — an on-demand platform to find, book, and manage access to 700+ coworking spaces across 100+ cities without long-term leases, with spend governance, policy controls, vendor consolidation, and usage-intelligence reporting. Founded in 2015. No public developer API is documented; a custom API and integrations are offered on the enterprise tier.

## Product
- [Homepage](https://getcroissant.com): Workspace access, spend governance, policy controls, vendor consolidation, and usage intelligence for distributed teams.
- [For Individuals](https://getcroissant.com/individual): On-demand workspace access for individual members.
- [For Workspaces / Partners](https://getcroissant.com/partners): Listing and partnership program for coworking space operators.
- [Pricing](https://getcroissant.com/pricing): Early Stage, Scaling (usage-based), and Enterprise (custom, includes custom integrations & API) tiers.
